# Voice and copy

All site copy lives in `src/lib/content/` as TypeScript, so a phrase is written once and reads the same everywhere. The values below are imported from there.

## Rules

- **One primary call to action.** Every primary button reads "{site.primaryCta}" and links to `#contact`. The secondary action is "See the case study".
- **Plain, confident, specific.** Say what Union EV does ("We design, install, operate and maintain…") rather than adjectives. Use real figures from the case study, and date them (`caseStudy.reportedAt`).
- **Spell out abbreviations on first use:** electric vehicle (EV), kilowatt (kW), kilowatt hours (kWh). The hero wraps "EV" in `<abbr>`.
- **Only claims the business can support.** Revenue share, install time and insurance are listed for confirmation in `docs/open-points.md`; "100% renewable" and "24/7 monitoring" were removed for this reason.
- **Title Case** for eyebrows, nav labels and the hero headline ("Who We Work With", "EV Charging for Your Site."); sentence case for section titles and body ("Survey to operation — we handle it all").
- **British English:** colour, organisation, licence.
- **Testimonials** carry a name and role only with permission; otherwise an anonymous attribution such as "Apsley Marina Resident".
