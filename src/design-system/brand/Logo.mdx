## Usage

- **Sizes:** 2.5rem tall in the nav (`size="nav"`, loaded eagerly), 2rem in the footer (`size="footer"`, lazy). Don't go below 2rem: the "CHARGING" line stops being legible.
- **On light backgrounds** (white, `--color-surface-alt`) use it as is.
- **On dark backgrounds** the green mark has too little contrast, so it sits on a white plate with `--space-1-5` × `--space-3` padding and `--radius-lg`, as in the footer. Don't recolour the SVG.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the plate padding clear around it; never crowd it with text.
- **Alt text** is "Union Electric Vehicle Charging". The SVG also carries its own `<title>`.
